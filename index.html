3&free

Do your three. Then, whatever you want. Pick three things each day. In the full version your chosen apps stay locked until they're done; otherwise a Shortcut you set up once sends you back here.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: 3andfree.app@gmail.com. We read everything and usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

How does the lock work? With Screen Time access (the full version), open the ••• menu and choose "lock your apps". You pick the apps with Apple's own picker, and they stay locked until all three of today's things are done, then open for 60 minutes. When you open a locked app, the shield says how many are left and shows your first open item in your own words (it appears only on your unlocked phone, when you open a blocked app; the widgets and the Live Activity show your items on the Lock Screen too). There is one free 15-minute emergency unlock a day: tap the status pill under your cards ("locked · 2 to go ›") and "need your apps now?" appears for about 10 seconds. It asks you to tap a reason (it’s urgent, someone needs me, or i just want to), and that reason stays on your phone. With 3&free Pro you can switch on the strict lock in the lock settings, which removes the emergency unlock; if Pro ends, the emergency unlock comes back. Without Screen Time access, the menu row is called "shortcut nudge": the app shows you how to set one Shortcut that sends you back here, and nothing is locked.

What does 3&free keep on my phone? Your three, their steps and notes, photos you attach to a note (and any files attached in earlier versions), and the days you have finished (up to 400 days of history). All of it stays on your iPhone and is never uploaded. To remove it, open the ••• menu, tap history, and use "delete all history and attachments" at the bottom (the size of your attachments is shown there too); your streak stays.

How do I put it on my Lock Screen? Add your first thing in the app and the Live Activity appears. You can also add the 3&free widgets: touch and hold the Lock Screen or Home Screen, tap Customize or Edit, and pick 3&free.

The morning reminder. Turn on the "morning reminder" switch in the ••• menu; a time you can adjust appears once it is on. It is up to four weeks of scheduled local notifications on your iPhone, re-planned when you open the app, and none on days your three are already written. After your very first 3/3, if it is off, one line offers "remind me tomorrow at 8am? ›", once. If notifications are off for 3&free in iOS, the row says so and an "open Settings" link takes you there. There is no push, and nothing is sent from a server.

Countdowns. Open the ••• menu, tap coming up, type a name (up to 40 characters) and pick tomorrow, 1 week, 2 weeks or 1 month, or step the days. The list shows your own words, like "essay · 14 days". Days are counted from the 4 a.m. start of your day; between midnight and 4 a.m. the app says it is still yesterday's three.

Themes and the week look-back. Sunset is the free theme. Ocean (deep navy, cyan glow) and neon (near-black, lime glow) are in 3&free Pro, in the app, widgets and Lock Screen. The weekly look-back is open to everyone (tap the streak line under your cards); sharing it as an image is Pro. After a 3/3 a quiet "share it ↗" link shares a card; the share sheet never opens by itself.

I don't see the Dynamic Island. It needs an iPhone 14 Pro or newer. On other iPhones the Live Activity shows on the Lock Screen.

The Lock Screen activity disappeared. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours. Open the app and it comes back.

Siri and Shortcuts. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with "Ask for Input" then "Open URL" threeandfree://add?text=[Provided Input]. Name it "Add to Three" and say "Hey Siri, Add to Three". You can also put it on the Action Button.

Restore Pro. Open the ••• menu, tap 3&free pro, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Delete my data. Delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
