Common questions

How do I put it on my Lock Screen? Add your first item in the app. If "Show on Lock Screen" is on, a Live Activity appears. You can also add the Today's Three widgets: touch and hold the Lock Screen or Home Screen, tap Customize or Edit, and pick Today's Three.

I don't see the Dynamic Island. It needs an iPhone 14 Pro or newer. On other iPhones the Live Activity shows on the Lock Screen.

The Lock Screen activity disappeared. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours. Open the app and it comes back.

Siri and Shortcuts. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with "Ask for Input" then "Open URL" todaysthree://add?text=[Provided Input]. Name it "Add to Three" and say "Hey Siri, Add to Three". You can also put it on the Action Button.

Restore Pro. Open the Pro row at the bottom of the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Delete my data. Delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
