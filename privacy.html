3&free: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

The short version: 3&free does not collect your data. Your three things, your countdowns and your streak stay on your iPhone. We never see them.

What the app stores

  • The up to three items you type each day, and whether you ticked them off.
  • Your "Coming up" countdowns (name, Homework or Exam, and date).
  • Your streak, your theme, whether you have Pro, the one on/off setting for the Lock Screen, and your lock choices (whether the lock is on, and how long apps open after you finish).

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone only. The app saves this inside its own private storage on your device.
  • Shared with the app's own widgets. A small copy (today's items, which are done, and your next countdown) is shared with the 3&free widgets, Live Activities and lock extensions on the same phone, so they can show it. It does not go anywhere else.
  • On your Lock Screen. When the Lock Screen option is on, your items appear in the Live Activity and Lock Screen widgets. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can read them, so don't type anything you want to keep private. You can turn this off with the "Show on Lock Screen" switch, and iOS lets you hide Lock Screen content until you unlock your phone.
  • iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or to a computer), iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That is Apple's feature and you control it in iOS Settings. We never receive it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account or sign-in. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking software. The app shows no ads, and it never sends your items, countdowns or streak anywhere. We do not sell or share data, because we have none.

The app lock (Screen Time)

Version 1.0 does not use Screen Time. Instead, the app shows you how to make one Shortcuts automation on your iPhone that opens 3&free when an app you pick is opened. Shortcuts keeps that automation; 3&free never sees it or which apps it uses. A later version may add the Screen Time lock described below.

If you turn on the optional lock, 3&free uses Apple's Screen Time features to block the apps you choose until your three things are done. You restrict only your own iPhone, and you can turn the lock off at any time.

  • You pick the apps in Apple's own picker. iOS keeps that choice on your iPhone as an opaque token. 3&free cannot see which apps you picked, and it never sees your Screen Time usage.
  • Nothing about your locked apps or your Screen Time is sent to us or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising.
  • The lock needs your permission, which you can decline. The rest of the app still works.
  • To remove everything the lock stored, turn the lock off in the app, or delete the app.

Buying Pro

3&free Pro is a subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial) handled by Apple. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription, and you can cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. We never see your payment details, and the app only asks Apple, on your device, whether Pro is active.

The morning reminder

The reminder is off until you turn it on, and iOS asks for notification permission only then. When it is on, your iPhone shows one local notification a day at the time you choose. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing leaves the device, we don't send it, and we don't know when or whether you use it. Turn it off in the app's menu, or in Settings → Notifications → 3&free.

Sharing your 3/3 card

When you tap Share, the app makes an image on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. Your items are hidden on the card unless you choose to show them. The image goes only where you send it.

Children and teens

3&free is made for teenagers and students (13+). It is not aimed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either. We do not run accounts, chat, social features or ads. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes everything the app stored on your phone, including the data shared with its widgets. Remove any backups through iOS Settings if you want those gone too. You can also clear a single item or countdown inside the app at any time.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you live somewhere with data protection rights (such as the EU, UK or California), those rights remain yours; contact us if you have a question.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we do not share it. You can ask us to delete it. Email: 3andfree.app@gmail.com

Apple

Apple may collect its own limited data when you download or use apps (for example App Store purchase records, or crash and usage reports if you chose to share them with developers in iOS Settings). That is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we only see anonymous summaries, if any.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. We will not start collecting data without changing this policy first.
