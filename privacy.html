3&free: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

The short version: 3&free does not collect your data. Your three things, your countdowns and your streak stay on your iPhone. We never see them.

What the app stores

  • The up to three items you type each day with their steps, notes and attachments, and whether you ticked them off.
  • History: the days you have used, kept on your iPhone (up to 400 days) with their items, steps, notes and attachments. The free version shows the last 7 days; Pro shows them all.
  • Your "Coming up" countdowns (a name and a date).
  • While the lock is on, the shield that appears when you open a blocked app shows how many of your three are left and the title of your first open item (your own words, never a note or a step), kept in the app group on your iPhone.
  • Your streak, your theme, whether you have Pro, your lock choices (whether the lock is on and whether strict lock is on).
  • If you use the emergency unlock, the reason you tapped (it's urgent, someone needs me, or i just want to), saved with that day. It stays on your iPhone, shows in your history and the week look-back, and is never sent anywhere.

Where it is stored

  • On your iPhone only. The app saves this inside its own private storage on your device.
  • Photos you attach to a note (and files attached in earlier versions) are copied into the app's private storage on your iPhone and stay there. They are never uploaded. A photo (or a file attached in an earlier version) is deleted from your phone when no card, kept day or carried-over item uses it any more, or when you use "delete all history and attachments" in the app's menu.
  • Shared with the app's own widgets. A small copy (today's items, which are done, and your next countdown) is shared with the 3&free widgets, Live Activities and lock extensions on the same phone, so they can show it. It does not go anywhere else.
  • On your Lock Screen. Your items appear in the Live Activity and Lock Screen widgets (turn Live Activities off for 3&free in iOS Settings if you prefer not). Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can read them, so don't type anything you want to keep private. There is no switch in the app: iOS Settings > 3&free > Live Activities turns it off, and iOS lets you hide Lock Screen content until you unlock your phone.
  • iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud Backup or to a computer), iOS may include the app's data in that backup. That is Apple's feature and you control it in iOS Settings. We never receive it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account or sign-in. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking software. The app shows no ads, and it never sends your items, countdowns or streak anywhere. We do not sell or share data, because we have none.

The app lock (Screen Time)

The full version of 3&free uses Apple's Screen Time features (the Family Controls framework) for the optional lock. A version without that access does not use Screen Time at all: it only shows you how to make one Shortcuts automation on your iPhone that opens 3&free when an app you pick is opened. Shortcuts keeps that automation; 3&free never sees it or which apps it uses.

If you turn on the optional lock in the full version, 3&free uses Apple's Screen Time features to block the apps you choose until your three things are done. You restrict only your own iPhone, and you can turn the lock off at any time.

  • You pick the apps in Apple's own picker. iOS keeps that choice on your iPhone as an opaque token. 3&free cannot see which apps you picked, and it never sees your Screen Time usage.
  • Nothing about your locked apps or your Screen Time is sent to us or to anyone else. It is not used for advertising.
  • With 3&free Pro you can switch on a strict lock, which removes the emergency unlock. That switch is stored on your iPhone only.
  • The lock needs your permission, which you can decline. The rest of the app still works.
  • To remove everything the lock stored, turn the lock off in the app, or delete the app.

Buying Pro

3&free Pro is a subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial) handled by Apple. Apple processes the payment and manages the subscription, and you can cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. We never see your payment details, and the app only asks Apple, on your device, whether Pro is active.

The morning reminder

The reminder is off until you turn it on, and iOS asks for notification permission only then. When it is on, the app schedules up to 28 local notifications on your iPhone (four weeks, one per morning), at the time you choose, and re-plans them when you open the app. It schedules none on days your three are already written, and the text can mention your streak. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing leaves the device, we don't send it, and we don't know when or whether you use it. Turn it off in the app's menu, or in Settings → Notifications → 3&free.

Sharing your 3/3 card

When you tap Share, the app makes an image on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. Your items are hidden on the card unless you choose to show them. The image goes only where you send it.

Children and teens

3&free is made for teenagers and students (13+). It is not aimed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to collect from children either. We do not run accounts, chat, social features or ads. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes everything the app stored on your phone, including the data shared with its widgets. Remove any backups through iOS Settings if you want those gone too. You can also clear a single item or countdown inside the app at any time. To remove every kept day and every photo and file but keep using the app, use "delete all history and attachments" in the menu; this deletes those records and files, while your streak stays.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you live somewhere with data protection rights (such as the EU, UK or California), those rights remain yours; contact us if you have a question.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, and we do not share it. You can ask us to delete it. Email: 3andfree.app@gmail.com

Apple

Apple may collect its own limited data when you download or use apps (for example App Store purchase records, or crash and usage reports if you chose to share them with developers in iOS Settings). That is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we only see anonymous summaries, if any.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. We will not start collecting data without changing this policy first.
